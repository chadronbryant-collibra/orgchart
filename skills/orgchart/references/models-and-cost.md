# Models and cost

Use Haiku for listing templates, CSV cleanup and charts under about 15 people.
Use Sonnet by default for building and updating decks. Reserve Opus for large
reorg planning when the extra reasoning is useful. Availability and cost depend
on the user's plan and setup.

Preview first, make one batched write, and avoid repeatedly reading whole decks.
Read only the objects needed to verify the change and reconcile uncertain writes.
